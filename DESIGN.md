## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the authoritative, reliable, and institutional precision of a premier cross-border customs brokerage. Built for cross-border vehicle nationalization, the visual narrative communicates regulatory legitimacy, prompt institutional compliance, and ironclad security.

### Emotional Response & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:** Mexican citizens, fleet operators, expatriates, and commercial vehicle importers seeking a legally foolproof, hassle-free importation process through Nuevo Laredo, Tamaulipas.
- **Emotional Response:** Absolute peace of mind, administrative certainty, regulatory authority, and swift operational competence.
- **Design Philosophy:** Modern Institutional Corporate with high-trust engineering. The interface avoids playful ambiguity, leaning into structured geometry, high-contrast legibility, calibrated financial/legal data presentation, and official certification aesthetics.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around federal authority, institutional trust, and high-conversion accents. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy Suite (`#0B1E36`, `#122B4D`, `#1A3B66`):** Governs structural headers, corporate navigation, dominant text, and official agency containers. Conveys institutional stability, legal authority, and border protocol compliance.
- **Secondary Amber/Gold Suite (`#D97706`, `#F59E0B`, `#B45309`):** Reserved exclusively for high-intent conversion paths—VIN verification triggers, quote calculators, pedimento validations, and urgent call-to-actions. 
- **Surface & Cool Grays (`#FFFFFF`, `#F4F6F9`, `#E2E8F0`):** Pure white serves as the primary canvas for document clarity, paired with cool stone foundations (`#F4F6F9`) for section alternating and input field backgrounds.
- **Muted Structural Slate (`#64748B`):** Applied to metadata, vehicle specification labels, legal disclaimers, and secondary supporting icons.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success / Legal Clearance: `#059669` (Pedimento Aprobado / Validated)
  - Warning / Document Review: `#D97706` (Trámite Pendiente)
  - Critical / Border Rejection: `#DC2626` (No Elegible / VIN Observado)

## Typography

The type scale combines the commanding architectural authority of **Montserrat** for display and section headers with the neutral, hyper-legible precision of **Inter** for forms, tables, and document disclosures.

### Font Hierarchy & Roles
- **Headlines (Montserrat):** Used in weights 600 (SemiBold) and 700 (Bold). Geometric and confident, it asserts administrative seniority and institutional history. Uppercase treatment is strictly limited to small system labels, table column headers, and legal category chips (`label-md`).
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Used in weights 400 (Regular) and 600 (SemiBold). Selected for high legibility across dense customs tariffs, VIN input fields, legal requirements checklists, and cost breakdown matrices.
- **Tabular Data:** All monetary, tax (IVA/IGI), and VIN codes must use monospace or tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within Inter to align currency tables precisely.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a structured 12-column fixed grid with responsive gutters and generous vertical breathing room to convey corporate stature and reduce visual fatigue during multi-step administrative processes.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max width `1200px` (`75rem`), centered with `2rem` gutters and `4rem` outer margin. Allows dual-column layouts for complex quote wizards: 7 columns for vehicle data inputs, 5 columns for the dynamic real-time tax breakdown card.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margin. Forms collapse into single-column flows with sticky footer summaries.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` edge margin. Focus moves to linear vertical progression, thumb-accessible primary action buttons, and clear document scanning cards.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on an 8pt baseline scale: `space-xl` (32px) separates distinct form sections, `space-3xl` (64px) separates informational landing modules, and `space-4xl` (96px) sets hero containers apart.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp, precision-engineered shadows combined with structural borders to evoke institutional security and digital permanence, avoiding ethereal blurs in favor of grounded physical depth.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and cool neutral `#F4F6F9` base layers. Zero shadow, framed with sharp 1px structural borders in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Used for trust proof cards, vehicle specification panels, and inspection guidelines.
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.08), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.08)`
  - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Quote Panels):** Used for dynamic tariff calculators, interactive VIN decoders, and prominent hover states.
  - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.04)`
  - Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Actions):** Used for official document viewers, pedimento PDF preview modals, and sticky conversion bars.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.08)`
  - Border: `1px solid #94A3B8`

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Border radii are restrained to maintain an official, high-trust corporate posture. Overly rounded or pill-shaped designs diminish the feeling of legal rigor.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Standard Controls & Inputs (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to form fields, primary buttons, dropdown triggers, and verification badge containers.
- **Cards & Data Modules (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to quotation breakdown containers, trust guarantee panels, and modal shells.
- **Badges & Official Seals (0.25rem / 4px):** Kept crisp and structured, mimicking customs stamps and border transit clearance decals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Amber Gold):** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600, border radius `0.25rem`. Hover state transitions to `#B45309`. Focus ring: `2px solid #F59E0B` with `2px` offset. Dedicated strictly to high-conversion actions: *"Cotizar Nacionalización"*, *"Validar VIN"*, *"Iniciar Trámite"*.
- **Institutional Primary (Navy):** Background `#0B1E36`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to `#1A3B66`. Used for formal operations like *"Descargar Pedimento"*, *"Consultar Requisitos"*.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #0B1E36`, text `#0B1E36`. Hover state fills with `#F4F6F9`.
- **Destructive:** Border and text `#DC2626`, background transparent. Hover fills `#FEF2F2`.

### Input Fields & VIN Search
- **Container:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0 1rem`.
- **Text & Placeholder:** Typography `body-md`. Placeholder color `#94A3B8`. Active text `#0B1E36`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#1A3B66` with a crisp `3px` glow of `rgba(26, 59, 102, 0.15)`.
- **VIN Specifier Input:** Monospaced letter spacing (`0.1em`), automatic uppercase conversion, paired with an integrated validation status chip (Checkmark in `#059669` or Alert in `#DC2626`) directly within the right-hand trailing slot.

### Cards & Institutional Proof Panels
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- **Accented Certification Card:** Features a top edge border accent (3px solid `#D97706` or `#0B1E36`) to spotlight customs guarantees, 20+ years experience proof points, and SAT/ANAM compliance credentials.

### Badges, Trust Seals & Customs Chips
- **Institutional Status Badge:** Height 24px, uppercase `label-md`, padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`, radius `0.25rem`.
  - *Aprobado / Importable:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
  - *Decreto / Regularización:* Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
  - *Restringido:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
- **Trust Seals:** Dual-tone lockup featuring customs agent credential markers (e.g., *"Patente Aduanal Autorizada"*, *"20+ Años en Nuevo Laredo"*), incorporating micro-shield icons, subtle `#0B1E36` borders, and security guilloche micro-patterns.

### Lists & Document Checklists
- **Checklist Items:** Stacked vertical list separated by `0.75rem`. Each item features a solid circular checkmark (`#059669`, 18px diameter) with an inset white check icon, followed by `body-md` typography. Required documentation notes (e.g., *"Título Original / Bill of Sale"*, *"Identificación Oficial INE"*) feature an inline `label-md` badge indicating mandatory vs. optional status.

### Specialized Component: Dynamic Quote & Tariff Matrix
- **Data Table / Breakdown:** Alternating striping using `#F4F6F9` on row hover. Numerical entries right-aligned with `tabular-nums`. Highlighting net agency fees, IGI (Impuesto General de Importación), DTA (Derecho de Trámite Aduanero), and IVA in distinct tonal rows, terminating with an emphatic summary card rendered in `#0B1E36` with `#F59E0B` total valuation.
